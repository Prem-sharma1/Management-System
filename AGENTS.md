# Client Deliverable Scheduling & Plan Duration Rules

1. **Always Respect Plan Duration**:
   - **1-Month (Monthly)**: 4 Weekly Reports over 4 weeks.
   - **3-Month**: 12 Weekly Reports over 12 weeks with full 3-month deliverable counts.
   - **6-Month**: 24 Weekly Reports over 24 weeks with full 6-month deliverable counts.
   - **Yearly / Annual (12-Month)**: 52 Weekly Reports over 52 weeks with full annual deliverable counts.

2. **Sunday Exclusions**:
   - Deliverable task dates MUST NEVER fall on a Sunday. If a calculated date lands on Sunday, automatically advance it to Monday.

3. **Dedicated Staff Ownership**:
   - **AI Video Scripts**: ALWAYS assigned to **Harshit** (`AI Video Lead`).
   - **Account Management & Reports**: The dedicated Social Media Executive handles Access Collection, Page/Account Setup, Ads Run, and ALL Weekly Reports for that account.
